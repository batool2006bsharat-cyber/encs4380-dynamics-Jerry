USEFUL METHODS

Tuning ideas

P: Kp

Often speeds up rise time, but too much can increase overshoot. P-only control generally leaves step steady-state error.

I: Ki

Accumulates error and can remove steady-state error. Large integral action can produce overshoot and oscillation.

D: Kd

Responds to the rate of the measured output and often reduces overshoot, but does not remove steady-state error by itself.
