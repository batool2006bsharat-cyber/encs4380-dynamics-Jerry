How damping shapes a second-order sensor

An accelerometer or a spring scale is a mass on a spring with some friction. ζ (damping ratio) decides how it settles: ring, creep, or arrive just in time. ωn (natural frequency) decides how fast: doubling it squeezes the whole curve into half the time without changing its shape.

Input is a 1 N step force, so the displacement settles at K = 1/k.
